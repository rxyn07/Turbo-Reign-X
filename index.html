ABOUT TURBO REIGN X

Turbo Reign X is a Valorant team focused on competition, improvement and building a strong community around our players. We believe good teams are made through discipline, trust and the willingness to keep learning.

Our organization currently fields four rosters: TRX Knights, TRX Power, TRX Mura and TRX Bind. TRX Knights serves as our main lineup, while every roster represents the same TRX identity.
